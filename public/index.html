Does this predict my future?

No, and anyone telling you otherwise is selling you something. A palm cannot tell you what will happen. What a reading can do is look closely at something you have carried your whole life and say something specific about it.

How is this different from a horoscope?

A horoscope stays vague so it can never miss, which is also why it never lands. This reads your actual photo and commits to what it sees, including the parts that are not flattering. If a sentence would be equally true of anyone else, it has failed.
