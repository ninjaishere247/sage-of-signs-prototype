The gap
Plainly

A palm cannot tell you what will happen. Anyone who says otherwise is selling you something.

What a reading can do is look closely at something you have carried your whole life and say something specific about it. Specific enough that it could be wrong.

Most readings hedge. They stay vague so they can never miss, which is also why they never land. Yours commits. It names what it sees in your hand, including the parts that are not flattering, and it tells you where two of your lines disagree with each other.

If that sounds more useful than a horoscope, it is meant to.
