Before you start

The honest answers.

Does this actually predict my future?

No, and anyone telling you otherwise is selling you something. A palm cannot tell you what will happen. What a reading can do is look closely at something you have carried your whole life and say something specific about it. Specific enough that it could be wrong.

How is this different from a horoscope?

A horoscope stays vague so it can never miss, which is also why it never lands. This reads your actual photo and commits to what it sees, including the parts that are not flattering. If a sentence in your reading would be equally true of anyone else, it has failed.

What happens to my photo?

Your photo is used to write your reading and nothing else. It is not published, not sold, and not shared with anyone.

Do I have to pay to see anything?

No. Your heart line is read in full and returned to you free, with a preview of the other three. You only pay if you decide you want the rest, and no card is needed to read the free part.

What if my photo is not good enough?

You will be told straight away and asked for another, rather than being handed a vague reading. Daylight and spread fingers are usually all it takes.
